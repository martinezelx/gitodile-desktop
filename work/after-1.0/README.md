# After 1.0.0

Approved tasks that are deliberately not scheduled before `1.0.0`. Tasks here
are `planned` or `blocked` and hold no queue position. When a post-`1.0.0`
version starts, its tasks move into that version's folder, get `status:
active` and a queue position. Unrefined ideas stay in
[`backlog.md`](../backlog.md).

The post-`1.0.0` themes (`1.1` safety net, `1.2` understand, `1.3` publish
with confidence, `1.4` parallel work, `2.x` working alongside agents) are
described in the [roadmap](../../docs/ROADMAP.md#after-100).
